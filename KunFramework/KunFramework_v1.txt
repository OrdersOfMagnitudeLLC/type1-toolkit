A TRIBUTE TO ALPHONSE DE POLIGNAC (1826–1863)
================================================================================

Alphonse de Polignac was born in 1826 to a French noble family. He studied at
Saint-Cyr, served in the military, and found time for mathematics. In 1849, at

primes can be. A foam surface at minimum curvature must, by self-similarity,
return to minimum curvature infinitely often. De Polignac's pairs are those
returns. They are not rare accidents. They are the geometry of the foam.

This one is for Alphonse de Polignac.
