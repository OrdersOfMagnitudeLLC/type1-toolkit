NSBVH8 vs Embree 4.4.1 (native build, -O3 -march=native): 100K rays, 10K AABBs, 25x20x1 grid (250-unit cells)
Confirmed 2026-09-13 on i7-1165G7.

Scene                              | Ray Type                  | NS rays/sec | Embree rays/sec | NS BVH8 (ms) | Embree (ms) | Ratio
-----------------------------------|--------------------------|-------------|-----------------|-------------|-------------|-----------
Dense Uniform (10K random, 500/500)| random                   | 14,372,553  | 22,837,823      | 6.96        | 4.38        | 1.59x slower
Sparse Clustered (20 clusters, 20/500)| 70% targeted + 30% random| 13,968,010 | 10,178,868      | 7.16        | 9.82        | 1.37x faster
Mixed (10 clusters + 5K random, 500/500)| 70% targeted + 30% random| 10,960,110 | 7,629,760      | 9.12        | 13.11       | 1.44x faster

Win condition: Clustered geometry — sparse clustered and mixed scenes with
targeted rays. NS-BVH grid skips empty cells (96% in sparse scene),
delivering 1.37-1.44x over Embree 4.4.1.
Dense uniform is a documented loss (1.59x slower): no structure to exploit,
Embree's SAH-optimized BVH2 wins on build quality for uniform distributions.
