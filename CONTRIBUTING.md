# Contributing to Orders of Magnitude

Welcome. Contributions are open: code, docs, testing, all of it.

## How to contribute
Fork the repo, create a branch, open a PR. Keep changes scoped and documented.

## The CLA
Before your PR merges, add this line to your PR description:

"I have read and agree to the OOM CLA."

That's it. No forms, no DocuSign.

The CLA exists so there's no legal ambiguity about what you're contributing and how OOM can use it. It protects you too: your ownership of what you wrote is explicitly on record. Everything you contribute stays free for individuals and nonprofits under the OOM license. Companies pay. Your work reaches the world.
