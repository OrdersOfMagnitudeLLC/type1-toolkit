IMPLICATIONS
================================================================================

This screen generates novel compositions from first principles and validates
them with the foam screener's Anderson-Debye phonon stability criterion.
Every candidate was synthesized in silico from element pools, then screened
using Vegard's-law-mixed elastic constants. No DFT was run.

The v2 screen fixes three problems from v1:
  1. All Heusler scores were identical (0.658): now Anderson-Debye gives
     real theta_D variation (720-814K) via per-formula B/G/rho from Vegard mixing
  2. Binary compounds (FeS2, NiS2, etc.) dominated: now bimetallic M1M2X2 only
  3. Sort was by theta_D, putting weak-stability high-Debye candidates first :
     now sorted by stability score (primary), theta_D (tiebreaker)

Key findings:
  - SEMICONDUCTOR: MnFeSe2 leads at score 0.593 (theta_D=458K): the highest
    stability score in the set, all elements >900ppm. MnFeS2 (0.590, 590K)
    and TiFeS2 (0.589, 581K) follow in the same tier. Te variants were
    filtered out (Te at 0.001 ppm crustal abundance is not earth-abundant).
    S variants have higher theta_D; Se variants have slightly higher scores.

  - MRAM: Fe2TiSi leads at theta_D=814K, score 0.658: ultra-abundant
    (Ti:6200ppm). The Ti/Mn-Y Heusler subfamily (score 0.658) dominates
    the Cr/V subfamily (score 0.508). Within each subfamily, theta_D is
    the ranking signal: 814K down to 720K within the 0.658 tier.

  - NOVELTY: 15 of 30 candidates are NOVEL, 15 are KNOWN (Materials Project
    cross-reference with normalized formula matching). The MP lookup was
    initially buggy: MP returns formula_pretty with alphabetically sorted
    elements (Co2MnSi → MnCo2Si), so exact string matching missed known
    compounds. Fixed with canonical normalization. Phonon validity is from
    the foam screener; novelty tracking is a separate question for
    publication.

================================================================================
RANKING METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Between subfamilies: ranked by stability score (foam mechanical criterion).
  - Score = isotropy x atom_count - d_penalty - EN_penalty - magnetic_penalty
  - Ti/Mn-Y Heuslers: 0.658 (no d-electron penalty: Ti d0, Mn d5 half-fill)
  - Cr/V-Y Heuslers:  0.508 (d-electron penalty 0.15 for partially filled d1-d4)

Within subfamilies: ranked by theta_D (Anderson-Debye acoustic phonon cutoff).
  - theta_D varies 30-80K within a score tier due to different B/G/rho
    from Vegard mixing of elemental elastic constants.
  - This is real physical discrimination: Fe2TiSi (B=137, G=69, theta_D=814K)
    vs Ni2MnAl (B=136, G=56, theta_D=720K).

Confidence: HIGH = Anderson-Debye with score >= 0.5; LOW = Anderson-Debye
with score < 0.5 (borderline, SPARC confirmation recommended).

================================================================================
SCORE DISCRIMINATION NOTES
================================================================================

Between subfamilies: ranked by stability score:
  The foam screener's mechanical score (isotropy x atom_count - d_penalty -
  EN_penalty - magnetic_penalty) is chemistry-driven and correctly identifies
  two magnetic-competition subfamilies in Heuslers:
    - 0.658: Y = Ti or Mn (no d-electron penalty: Ti d0, Mn d5 half-fill)
    - 0.508: Y = Cr or V (d-electron penalty 0.15 for partially filled d1-d4)
  All 15 MRAM candidates above are in the 0.658 tier (Ti/Mn-Y). The Cr/V-Y
  subfamily (Fe2CrSi, Co2CrSi, etc., score 0.508) was pushed below the top 15
  by the score sort. They remain valid candidates at lower confidence.

Within subfamilies: ranked by theta_D:
  Anderson-Debye captures per-formula B/G/rho from Vegard mixing, giving
  30-80K theta_D spread within a score tier. This is the ranking signal
  within a subfamily. Fe2TiSi (B=137, G=69, theta_D=814K) vs Ni2MnAl
  (B=136, G=56, theta_D=720K): same score, 94K theta_D difference from
  different elastic constants.
