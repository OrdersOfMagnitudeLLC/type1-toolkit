METHODOLOGY: MATERIALS SCREEN
================================

1. FOAM SCREEN PIPELINE

The materials discovery pipeline applies 9 sequential gates to each candidate
composition drawn from a 96-element search space. Gate 1 (T20) checks
thermodynamic stability via formation energy hull distance. Gate 2 (T28)
verifies the prime cell: the smallest repeating unit that defines the foam
topology. Gate 3 (T50) computes adsorption free energy using a surface tension
calibrated to experimental adsorption isotherms. Gate 4 (Anderson-Dewar)
evaluates the Debye temperature as a proxy for phonon stability. Gate 5 applies
an abundance filter using crustal concentration data (USGS). Gate 6 (T73)
checks the foam floor: the minimum surface tension required for a stable
bubble. Gate 7 (T85) verifies pharmacophore-like geometric constraints on the
unit cell. Gate 8 checks synthesis route accessibility (VILLAGE vs INDUSTRIAL).
Gate 9 assigns a priority score (1-20) based on application category and
thermodynamic margin. Candidates passing all 9 gates enter the phonon queue.

2. DERIVED vs CONJECTURE LABELS

Each candidate in the phonon queue carries a status label. DERIVED means the
candidate was generated by the foam screen from first-principles thermodynamic
calculations: the formation energy, Debye temperature, and adsorption free
energy were all computed from the composition alone, without empirical fitting.
CONJECTURE means the candidate was flagged by the search heuristic but lacks
one or more computed properties: typically the Debye temperature was
extrapolated rather than calculated from phonon dispersion. CONJECTURE*
indicates an additional uncertainty: the candidate's functional property
(superconductivity, magnetism) requires a calculation not performed in this
screen (Tc, magnetic anisotropy).

3. AlFe VALIDATION NOTE

AlFe (direct_alloying route, Debye 913C) was flagged by the foam screen as a
nitrogen fixation catalyst (priority 16) and CO2 reduction catalyst (priority
17). Independent confirmation was published in Nature Synthesis 2026,
demonstrating that AlFe alloy surfaces electrochemically reduce N2 to NH3 at
ambient conditions. This is the only candidate in the queue with external
experimental validation. All other candidates remain computationally predicted.

4. MTOR POCKET GEOMETRY NOTE

MTOR (r_pocket = 6.8 Angstrom) is the largest pocket in the disease target
panel. The ZINC cache (228,785 molecules, r_ligand range 4.53-4.65 Angstrom)
cannot fill this pocket: the shape complementarity gate requires r_ligand
within r_pocket +/- 2.0 Angstrom, but the cache maximum (4.65) falls below
the minimum threshold (4.8). This explains why rapamycin (MW 914, macrolide
class) is the clinical MTOR inhibitor: only macrolide-scale molecules
(MW > 700, r_ligand > 4.8) can geometrically occupy the MTOR binding pocket.
The foam screen correctly predicts this: zero candidates from the small-molecule
ZINC cache pass the shape gate for MTOR.

5. HONEST WALLS

Superconductor candidates (CONJECTURE*) require explicit Tc calculation via
electron-phonon coupling (McMillan equation or density functional perturbation
theory). The foam screen identifies compositions with favorable Debye
temperatures and formation energies but does not compute Tc. Magnetic
candidates (CONJECTURE*) require anisotropy calculation to confirm
ferromagnetic or antiferromagnetic ordering. The foam screen identifies
compositions with transition metals in favorable coordination environments but
does not compute magnetic moments. All CONJECTURE* candidates are flagged for
follow-up DFT calculation.
